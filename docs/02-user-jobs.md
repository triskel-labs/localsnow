# 02 — User Jobs

Derived from: `01-product-promise.md`

## Purpose

This layer names the jobs users need LocalSnow to do. These are not features yet.

## Client jobs

A client needs to:

- find lesson options in a resort;
- understand who is credible and eligible enough to trust;
- understand what sport/level/service they offer;
- know whether the option is open to receiving requests, without mistaking that for live availability;
- choose between direct inquiry and protected LocalSnow help;
- submit a request quickly on mobile;
- understand who will contact them, through which channel, and roughly when;
- understand what happens if the requested professional cannot serve them.

## Independent instructor jobs

An independent instructor needs to:

- look credible online;
- show where and what they teach;
- communicate language, credentials, experience, style and eligibility/trust markers;
- publish simple lesson offers;
- receive qualified requests;
- avoid heavy admin/calendar/software work in v1;
- control what is public.

## School/light provider jobs — conditional

A school or small provider may need to:

- appear credible in resort discovery;
- publish simple offers;
- receive requests;
- avoid staff management and internal operations software in v1.

This branch is conditional. Before surface mapping, Moli must choose one:

1. independent instructors only for the first map;
2. light school/provider profiles included with no staff/admin features;
3. schools deferred entirely.

## Operator/Moli jobs

Moli needs to:

- see incoming direct/protected requests;
- know which requests are direct demand signals versus protected cases needing action;
- know who the client wants and what they need;
- contact client/professional manually;
- record whether clarification is needed, professional has been contacted, response is pending, replacement may be needed, price approval may be needed, or the request is closed;
- track next action;
- keep internal notes;
- learn which manual steps repeat before automating them.

The exact state model comes later. These are business words only, not database/status names yet.

## Open decisions that block the surface map

- First launch market/resort and first ICP.
- Direct inquiry channel: email, WhatsApp, both, or platform notification later.
- Professional setup mode: self-serve, Moli-assisted, manual/invite, or hybrid.
- School/light-provider branch.
- Protected payment boundary.
- Minimum credibility/eligibility requirements.
- Safe response-window promise.

## What this layer does not decide

This document does not decide:

- page map;
- database schema;
- design components;
- SEO silos;
- payment implementation;
- engineering architecture.

Those come later.
