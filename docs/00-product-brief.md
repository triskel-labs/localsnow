# 00 — Product Brief

## Decision

LocalSnow starts fresh as a greenfield product. The legacy repo is an archive/reference, not a product or engineering source of truth.

## One-line product

LocalSnow helps clients find credible snowsports professionals and submit direct or protected lesson requests, while Moli manually coordinates protected fulfillment until real operations justify automation.

## Why this exists

Clients searching for ski/snowboard lessons face fragmented supply, unclear trust, unclear availability and confusing booking/contact paths. Professionals need credible visibility and qualified requests without adopting heavy admin software.

LocalSnow v1 should make the public side feel polished and safe while keeping the operational side intentionally manual.

## Primary users

1. **Client** — wants a lesson in a resort and needs a clear next step.
2. **Independent instructor** — wants a credible public profile and qualified requests.
3. **School/small provider** — may need a public profile and offers, but not staff-management software in v1.
4. **Operator/Moli** — needs a simple queue to manually fulfill protected requests.

## Product promise

### Free/direct path

LocalSnow helps clients discover and contact professionals directly. LocalSnow does not guarantee response, availability, replacement or refund on the direct path.

### Protected path

LocalSnow helps the client make the lesson happen. LocalSnow first tries the requested professional/school. If unavailable, LocalSnow helps reschedule, find a suitable replacement, or refund/no-charge depending payment state. If a replacement costs more, the client approves first.

## Revenue thesis

The first revenue proof is protected request/booking support, not ads, featured placement, paid profiles, sponsorships or complex commissions.

Instructor/school payout can stay manual until repeated real transactions prove automation is worth building.

## V1 success criteria

- A client can find a relevant professional and submit a request on mobile.
- A professional can publish a credible profile and simple offers.
- Moli can manually process incoming protected requests without guessing context.
- SEO route structure supports the first market without generating thin page bloat.
- The app does not overpromise instant booking or exact availability.

## Explicit non-goals

Not v1:

- instant booking;
- full marketplace;
- Stripe Connect;
- automated payouts;
- Google/iCal two-way sync;
- complex packages/promos/pricing engine;
- school staff management;
- SkiRelay private coordination;
- automatic replacement matching;
- full CRM/admin suite.

## Open founder decisions

- First launch market/resort.
- Language strategy.
- Whether schools are light v1 profiles or deferred.
- Whether protected payment is manual after qualification or built as a disabled Stripe boundary.
